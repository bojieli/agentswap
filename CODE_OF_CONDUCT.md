# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes bug reports, documentation improvements, tests, and new
harness adapters. The [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains the
technical boundaries; this page explains how we work together.

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not
about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work: what it does, what it breaks, what it costs.
- Accept feedback the same way. A rejected patch is a decision about a change,
  not a verdict on you.
- Assume good faith when a message reads badly. Most people here are writing in
  their second or third language, and terseness is not hostility.
- Respect a maintainer's "no". This project has a deliberately narrow scope,
  and most declined features are declined for reasons written down in the
  README.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone or their
background, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing others' private information,
and sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at the email on
[github.com/bojieli](https://github.com/bojieli). Reports stay private.

What happens next depends on what happened: usually a private word, sometimes
an edited or deleted comment, and for anything deliberate or repeated, a ban.
The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided to the person who
reported it.

Enforcement is not a court. Nobody here is owed a hearing, and "but technically
the rule says" is not an argument that will help.
